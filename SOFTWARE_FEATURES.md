# Software Features

This document describes the application from a user's point of view: what the app does and why it is useful.

## What the app does

Playlist Predator turns a YouTube playlist into a clean, trackable study and watch experience. Instead of treating a playlist like a long, messy list of videos, it shows how much time it really takes and lets you manage your progress with clear states.

## User-facing features

- Analyze any YouTube playlist from a link or playlist ID.
- See total video count, total duration, remaining time, and speed-based time estimates.
- Mark videos as done, skipped, or set them back to rewatch.
- Keep progress even if you refresh the page.
- Use the app without logging in.
- Sign in to sync progress across devices.
- Save playlists automatically when you open them while signed in.
- Browse your saved playlists from the sidebar.
- Search saved playlists quickly.
- Recover your account with forgot-password and reset-password flows.
- Use the app offline with cached assets and an offline fallback page.
- Switch between light and dark mode.
- Access an admin dashboard if your account is configured with admin access.

## Typical experience

1. Paste a YouTube playlist link or ID on the home page.
2. The app analyzes the playlist and shows the total time and per-video breakdown.
3. Track what you have done, skipped, or want to rewatch.
4. Sign in if you want your progress and saved playlists to follow you across devices.

## Benefits

- Saves time by summarizing long playlists up front.
- Makes it easier to finish study playlists and watch queues.
- Reduces lost progress by keeping state locally and in the cloud.
- Works well for guests and signed-in users alike.
